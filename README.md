# skeptic043

I enjoy putting together mods and useful tools, with a primary focus on small systems focused game mods and companion software. Most of my public work is for Sailwind, alongside Spectari, a standalone screen-sharing app, and mods for a few other games.

## Featured projects

- [Spectari](https://github.com/Skeptic043/spectari) — Stream a window or monitor over Tailscale or a local network with no account required.
- [Sailwind Chartroom](https://github.com/Skeptic043/sailwind-chartroom) — A desktop charting desk for Sailwind on Windows and macOS. Mark islands, plot routes, narrow down destinations from mission directions, follow your boat live with [Voyage Recorder](https://github.com/Skeptic043/sailwind-voyage-recorder), and share worlds, routes and races on [Chartroom Voyages](https://skeptic043.github.io/sailwind-chartroom-voyages/).
- [Sailwind HelmVR](https://github.com/Skeptic043/Sailwind-HelmVR) — Play Sailwind in PC VR on top of UUVR, with physical cargo grabbing, winches, wheel and tiller steering, and chart table interaction.
- [Sailwind Radio](https://github.com/Skeptic043/Sailwind-Radio) — A physical in-game radio and speakers for Sailwind that play your local music with 3D spatial audio.
- [New Beginnings](https://github.com/Skeptic043/sailwind-new-beginnings) — Customize or randomize your Sailwind starting port, boat, money, regional reputation, and starting equipment, including detected modded gear.
- [Scrambled Seas World Generator](https://github.com/Skeptic043/scrambled-seas-world-generator) — Generate custom Scrambled Seas worlds for Sailwind, up to the whole globe.
- [Survivor Leveling & Advancement](https://github.com/Skeptic043/survivor-leveling-and-advancement) — A progression and leveling mod for Project Zomboid.
